# Data Visualisation

## Project Description : 

Have you ever asked yourself how a country’s economic development and population characteristics influence its ecological footprint and environmental sustainability?

In this project, we explore data from multiple countries to uncover the links between economic performance, demographic features, and environmental impact. Using Python and data visualization tools, we transform data into insights that reveal how growth and population trends shape sustainability outcomes across the world.

## Our objectives :

- To examine how economic development (e.g. GDP, income levels) correlates with ecological indicators.

Why? To understand whether growth promotes or hinders sustainability.

- To analyze population characteristics (e.g. density, urbanization, age distribution) and their influence on environmental outcomes.

Why? Demographics often drive energy use, consumption patterns, and policy needs.

- To derive actionable insights:

Which countries are ecological outliers?
