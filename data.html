About the Dataset

The dataset we used for our analysis was taken from Kaggle made by the Global Footprint Network. The data is from 2016. We have updated and modified the csv file to adapat it to the project we are working on.

The ecological footprint measure was conceived by Mathis Wackernagel and William Rees at the University of British Columbia. Ecological footprint data was provided by the Global Footprint Network.

Here is the link of the full dataset below
Ecological Footprint Dataset

The dataset has 189 observations which correlates to the 189 countries in the dataset with 21 different Variables, ranging from the population of a country to the cropland.
